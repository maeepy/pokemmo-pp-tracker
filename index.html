PokeMMO PP Tracker

Search a Pokémon, pick one of its Gen 5 PokeMMO calc sets, and change any move from its dropdown. Tap − each time a move is used. PP values are Gen 5 base values; check them against the game.

Import sets

Paste sets in Pokémon Showdown format (the same text the Smogon calc and Showdown teambuilder export). Moves the tracker doesn’t know are skipped and listed.
